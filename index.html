What the equipment supports

What this equipment means for your build.

The specifications above translate into a small number of practical manufacturing capabilities. These are the ones customers ask about most often.

Parallel production lines

Two printers, two reflow ovens, and two AOI systems allow prototype and production work to run at the same time. Scheduled maintenance on one line does not stop the other.

High-mix changeover

120 feeders per placement machine, plus docking carts, allow several jobs to remain set up at once. Automatic splicing keeps the line running through reel changes.

Large-format boards

Single-lane placement supports boards from 50 × 40 mm up to 1,200 × 510 mm, with reflow accepting widths to 559 mm — relevant to backplanes, lighting boards, and long industrial assemblies.

Hidden-joint verification

Two X-ray systems support first-article checks and BGA void analysis. A dedicated rework station allows devices to be removed, reballed, and replaced without scrapping the assembly.

Controlled thermal profiles

Nine heated zones top and bottom, with reduced-nitrogen operation, support leaded and lead-free assemblies across mixed thermal masses. Maximum process temperature is 350 °C, with a 400 °C option.

In-house aqueous cleaning

Defluxing after reflow and selective soldering supports ionic-cleanliness requirements and prepares assemblies where conformal coating is specified.
